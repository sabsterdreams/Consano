Founder & Creator

Hi, I'm Sabina

I'm a university student who was motivated to create Consano from my own personal experiences with emotional abuse and bullying. Having attended an all-girls school, I know firsthand the struggles many young girls face when it comes to their friendships, especially when those friendship issues start leaning into something much more serious than simple fallouts.

The Resource I Wished For

I wanted to create the resource I wish I had when I was younger. A dedicated place for teens where the difficult emotions that arise from these issues could be validated, and where you can receive practical advice on how to grow through these experiences.

The Aware Platform

I created Aware (located in the resources section) to serve as a medium to educate young people on different forms of emotional abuse. It demonstrates how minor behaviors can escalate using relatable scenarios, reflective prompts, and interactive quizzes.

Stronger, Healthier Connections

My hope is that this website helps young girls feel supported and educated so that they can build stronger, healthier connections, set firm boundaries, and protect their mental well-being.
