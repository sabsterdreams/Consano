Introducing Consano

Because friendship shouldn't be this confusing

In a world of eye rolls, arguments and passive aggressive remarks, teenage friendship can feel like a whirlwind. But it's more than 'drama', and you know it. Friendships are a massive part of your life, especially at school. And that's exactly why you deserve to know what's healthy, what behaviours to tolerate and how to deal with being mistreated.

You're not meant to go through this alone

‘There's a girl sat in an anti-bullying assembly beside someone who makes her feel worthless everyday.’

We provide tools for healing, self-protection, and long-term emotional growth.

Read our Articles

Our articles cover a range of topics including jealousy, reputation damage and manipulative reactions. Learn more about the signs of unhealthy friendships and understand more about how to equip yourself with the tools required when dealing with emotional abuse.

Learn with our Resources

Whether you want to improve your emotional intelligence with our Aware educational platform, or want to clear up misunderstandings on buzzwords in the toxic friendship space, Consano has many resources that can help you.

Get the help you need

While these resources and articles aren't a substitute for professional support, they are made with the intention to make you feel validated and empowered to take those next steps in your confidence journey.
