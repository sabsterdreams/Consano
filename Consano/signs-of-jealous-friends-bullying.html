Red Flags & Hidden Signs Your Friend Might Be Jealous of You

"They're jealous of you"

It’s a common saying, but often times it can be true. Jealous friends are everywhere, and they are the worst kind—slowly chipping away at your confidence and making you question whether you are going insane for feeling offended.

When these behaviors start working together, they can be incredibly distracting, pulling your focus away from important goals like your studies and personal growth. Remember: **this behavior is largely out of your control.**

What to Do

Other than distancing yourself from these types of people, stop sharing your goals and achievements with them. Unsupportive people use your success as fuel for their envy. Save your good news for those who truly support you.

Jealous people often want social groups to buy into a fantasy where they are at the top. They view your achievements as direct threats to their status.

Don't Dim Your Light

True friends will celebrate and admire you for your positive traits rather than try to put you down. Gravitate toward people who lift you up rather than holding onto friendships that tear you down.
